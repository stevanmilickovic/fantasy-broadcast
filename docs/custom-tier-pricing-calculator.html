Cost and usage assumptions

Change these to explore risk. Hosting allocation and EUR generation reserves are planning assumptions. The server's unused traffic allowance must be shared across all leagues.

Concurrent slots do not bound distinct visits. Each modeled cold visit loads the full initial snapshot and every team emblem. Pagination/caching can reduce this.

Other assumptions: €1/TB outgoing overage; €0.06/GB/month storage reserve; 5% transaction fee on customer payment; €4.40 voice + €0.06 text reserve when AI is selected; 51.2 MB audio/viewer; 25% additional buffer on direct costs. USD 0.50 is Paddle's published fixed fee, not necessarily €0.50 on settlement. No fixed provider-plan fee is included; add its allocated share to hosting if applicable. Support, refunds, FX charges and taxes on costs may require higher reserves.
